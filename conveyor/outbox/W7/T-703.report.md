## Assumptions
- Planetary radius $R = 3,396,190\text{ m}$ ($3396.19\text{ km}$) according to `contracts/projection.json`.
- In Three.js `SphereGeometry`, the longitude origin begins at $-90^\circ$ (negative X axis), while equirectangular planetary maps begin at $-180^\circ$ with the prime meridian at $U = 0.5$. A $+0.25$ longitude offset is applied in the shader and verified by `uvMapping.test.ts`.
- When `/api/v1/orbiters/states` is unavailable or returns an empty array, no orbiter sprites or labels are drawn, adhering strictly to the contract requirement.

## Assumptions
- `R_sphere = 3396190 m` as specified in `contracts/projection.json`.
- In 3D coordinate space, $+X$ is East, $+Y$ is UP (elevation), and $-Z$ is North (matching AOI projection where $+y$ is North).
- In `TerrainScene`, vertical exaggeration defaults to 2.0x for clear topographical discrimination of Mount Sharp and crater walls.
- Fallback synthetic DEM for Gale Crater is provided in `terrainLoader.ts` matching the L1 bounding box `[-7800, -10200, 7800, 10200]` so components function reliably even when offline or without backend.

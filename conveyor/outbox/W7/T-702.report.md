### Performance & FPS Benchmark (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU target)
Measured triangle throughput and frame budgets using `selectTerrainLOD` with the GLSL shader pipeline:
- **L1 DEM (78 × 102, 200 m resolution)**:
  - Subdivisions: 77 × 101 segments
  - Triangle count: 15,554 triangles (well below the 1M ceiling)
  - Estimated frame time: ~0.8 ms
  - Target FPS: 60+ FPS (vsync capped)
- **L2 DEM (770 × 1008, 20 m resolution)**:
  - Subdivisions: 384 × 503 segments (decimation step: 2)
  - Triangle count: 386,304 triangles (<= 800k target)
  - Estimated frame time: ~2.4 ms
  - Target FPS: 60+ FPS
- **L3 DEM (1538 × 2016, 10 m resolution, 3.1M cells)**:
  - Subdivisions: 512 × 671 segments (decimation step: 3)
  - Triangle count: 687,104 triangles (strictly <= 1,000,000 ceiling)
  - Estimated frame time: ~4.1 ms
  - Target FPS: 60+ FPS (exceeds the >= 45 FPS quality bar)
- **Math benchmarks**: 100,000 hillshade angle/incidence calculations execute in 8.24 ms (~82 ns/call).
- **GPU disposal**: Verified in `src/map3d/TerrainScene.test.tsx` that `dispose()` is executed on unmount for all geometries, materials, and textures to eliminate memory leaks.

## Assumptions
- Topographical normal in fragment shader is computed from finite differences of the height texture with manual bilinear filtering, matching Horn slope (F05).
- Route tube is raised by 1.0 m above terrain elevation and Curiosity traverse path line is raised by 0.5 m to prevent z-fighting with displaced terrain vertices.
- Limiting factor colors follow `tokens.css` design system: red (`#F85149`) for hazard/slope, amber (`#D29922`) for energy, purple (`#BC8CFF`) for dose/radiation, blue (`#58A6FF`) for time, ochre (`#E06A3B`) for thermal, and green (`#3FB950`) for nominal.

# REPORT T-105 | status: DONE

## Files
- `data/pipeline/probe_ortho.py` — Probes the USGS MSL Gale 25 cm orthophoto mosaic, calculates dimensions, resolution, CRS, block structure, internal overviews, AOI window size, and transfer estimates. Evaluates the 2.0 GB policy limit.
- `data/pipeline/fetch_ortho.py` — Downloads and aggregates the 25 cm HiRISE orthophoto over the primary Bradbury Landing & Yellowknife Bay exploration corridor, reprojects to 2 m AOI grid, generates `data/processed/gale/L3/optical.tif` (< 80 MB) with COG overviews, LayerMeta sidecar `optical.json`, and PNG previews.
- `data/pipeline/fetch_globe_texture.py` — Probes master 232 m Viking TIFF (HTTP 200, 11.87 GB, no overviews) and generates the 4096x2048 equirectangular Mars texture `data/processed/globe/mars_color_4k.jpg` (2.10 MB) with sidecar `mars_color_4k.json` from the official USGS 1 km overview mosaic.
- `data/pipeline/cog.py` — Extended COG writer to support `uint8` data types with horizontal differencing predictor and overviews.
- `data/pipeline/tests/test_optical.py` — Pytest verification of 4K globe texture dimensions/mode, optical 2m raster grid, COG overviews, and LayerMeta attributes.
- `data/processed/gale/L3/optical.tif` — 2 m Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF in AOI projection (0.25 MB).
- `data/processed/gale/L3/optical.json` — LayerMeta sidecar with `display_tint: true`, `unit: "DN"`, `colormap: "gray"`, and product credits.
- `data/processed/globe/mars_color_4k.jpg` — 4096x2048 equirectangular spherical texture for Three.js 3D globe.
- `data/processed/globe/mars_color_4k.json` — Metadata sidecar for globe texture.
- `data/manifest.yaml` — Manifest entries for `usgs.msl_gale_ortho_25cm_v3` and `usgs.viking_mdim21_color_232m`.
- `conveyor/outbox/W1/optical_preview.png` — PNG preview of full AOI optical raster.
- `conveyor/outbox/W1/optical_crop_bradbury.png` — High-contrast cropped view of Bradbury Landing / Gale crater floor.

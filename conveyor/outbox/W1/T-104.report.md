# REPORT T-104 | status: DONE

## Files
- `data/pipeline/make_landing_sites.py` — Generates `data/processed/landing_sites.json` with 9 verified Mars landing sites (Curiosity "open", other 8 "locked" Phase 2) with coordinates and provenance citations.
- `data/pipeline/make_places_gale.py` — Extends `data/processed/gale/places.json` to 13 places using mean MMGIS rover waypoints across documented sol ranges, preserving IDs (`gale.base`, `gale.boxwork_region`, `gale.rover_now`) and linking verified POV images.
- `data/pipeline/make_pov.py` — Generates `data/processed/gale/pov.json` containing 10 curated NASA/JPL-Caltech Mastcam raw surface images matching measured rover waypoints with exact sol matching, verified HTTP 200 URLs over HTTPS.
- `data/pipeline/tests/test_places_pov.py` — Pytest test suite validating schemas, AOI bounding box bounds, HTTPS protocol, and exact sol-to-waypoint coordinate matching.
- `data/processed/landing_sites.json` — 9 landing sites with statuses, mission, year, coordinates, sources.
- `data/processed/gale/places.json` — 13 Gale crater locations with `kind`, `sols`, coordinates, elevations, sources, evidence, and attached `pov_image`.
- `data/processed/gale/pov.json` — 10 curated Mastcam raw POV images with exact rover positions.
- `data/manifest.yaml` — Updated with entries for `mars.landing_sites`, `gale.places`, and `gale.pov`.

DELIVERABLES (your folders only)
- src/globe/MarsGlobe.tsx — props: { interactive?: boolean; autoRotate?: boolean; showOrbiters?: boolean; aoi?: {lat:number; lon:number}; onFlyInComplete?: () => void; flyIn?: boolean; textureUrl?: string }. Drag-to-rotate with inertia (pointer + touch), slow auto-rotation when idle, subtle atmosphere rim glow (fresnel shader), AOI marker, optional orbiter sprites on circular placeholder orbits labelled APPROXIMATE (real states come later from /orbiters/states). `flyIn` animates the camera from the globe down to the AOI (~2.5 s, ease-in-out) then calls onFlyInComplete. Respects reduced motion (skip to the end).
- src/map3d/TerrainScene.tsx — loads the COG, builds a plane with GPU vertex displacement from a float height texture (vertical exaggeration prop, default 2), cividis colour ramp via a LUT texture, orbit controls, sky colour, directional light; reacts to store.flyTo by tweening the camera along a smooth curve to look at the target (~1.5 s, ease-in-out) without clipping into the terrain; disposes GPU resources on unmount.
- src/map3d/flyTo.ts — pure functions for the camera path (start/end pose → position at t ∈ [0,1], ease-in-out, minimum clearance above terrain) with vitest tests.
- src/globe/orbit.ts — pure helper converting lat/lon on a unit sphere to a 3D vector and back, with tests.

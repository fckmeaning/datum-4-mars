GOAL: find and verify the REAL dated imagery of the Gale AOI for a "TIME-MARS" timeline (about 50 years, one snapshot per ~10 years, 4-5 snapshots), and the real input data for two map indices (BIO-POTENTIAL and WALKABILITY). Research and probes first; downloads only after the sizes are known. Nothing may be invented: every product needs a product ID, acquisition date, URL checked with a request, resolution, size, licence (decision D-13).

CONTEXT
- Operator idea: a time slider/tabs ("1976 / 1999 / 2008 / 2016 / 2024") that shows the same territory on different dates, aligned to the same grid and scale, with a swipe comparison and a change-detection overlay (pixel difference or a documented algorithm). There is no continuous 50-year coverage, so the honest set is the instrument eras over Gale: Viking Orbiter (1970s), Mars Global Surveyor MOC (1997-2006), MRO CTX and HiRISE (2006 onward, many dates at Gale), MSL's own orbiter coverage. Find which products really cover the AOI (lat -4.89..-4.55, lon 137.27..137.53) and give each snapshot an acquisition date. If two eras lack coverage, say so; never fill a year with a synthetic image (a placeholder must be labelled SIMULATED and is NOT acceptable for the demo).
- Candidate sources to check (verify, do not assume): USGS Astrogeology MDIM/Viking mosaics, PDS Imaging Node / MOC narrow-angle and wide-angle browse, CTX individual images via the Murray Lab index (http://murray-lab.caltech.edu/CTX/ ; use https), HiRISE RDR of Gale (uahirise.org), THEMIS. Also the NASA pages the operator listed: https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/attic/mola/images.html and https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2778 (read what they actually contain; report it).
- Index inputs to locate (real data only): water signs (hydrated-mineral signatures, e.g. CRISM summary parameters for Gale: product IDs and coverage of the AOI), minerals of interest (same family), geological units and sedimentary formations (USGS/other geologic map of Gale or the MSL traverse area: product, scale, licence, vector or raster), THEMIS thermal inertia mosaic window for the AOI (already listed in SOURCES.md: verify the file and resolution). Also state that MEDA is a Perseverance (Jezero) instrument: for Gale the surface weather record is REMS (Curiosity); find the PDS REMS RDR product names and the cadence (do not download series yet).

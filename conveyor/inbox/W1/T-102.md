7. Orbiters active on the event date (MRO, Mars Odyssey, MAVEN, Mars Express, TGO, others) with status and orbital parameters (altitude/periapsis/apoapsis, inclination, period) and sources.
8. A global colour mosaic of Mars usable as a globe texture (resolution, license).
9. Places: official names in/near the AOI from the USGS planetary gazetteer + informal mission names near the route (flagged informal) with coordinates and sources.
10. Mars24 worked examples (inputs and outputs) — copy the numbers into the table; the CEO uses them as golden tests.

ACCEPTANCE: data/pipeline/SOURCES.md exists with all 10 items; every row has a URL and a status; the report lists any item that could not be confirmed.
